Coverage with few or no health questions.

Our specialty is guaranteed-issue and simplified-issue coverage. For someone with a health history, it can be the way to get covered at all.

  • Guaranteed-issue: no health questions to qualify
  • Simplified-issue: a short set of questions, no exam
  • Offered through work, during enrollment
