Plans that pay you, not the hospital.

Supplemental plans pay cash benefits directly to you, on top of your health insurance. These are the plans we offer most.

Accident

Cash for covered injuries, from an emergency room visit to a broken bone, to use however you need.

Cancer

Helps with the costs health insurance leaves behind during treatment, paid directly to you.

Critical illness

A lump-sum benefit after a covered diagnosis such as a heart attack or stroke.

Hospital

Pays you for a covered hospital admission and stay, on top of your health plan.

Short-term disability

Replaces part of your paycheck when a covered illness or injury keeps you out of work.

Life insurance

Term and permanent coverage, shopped across many carriers for the right fit and price.

Dental and vision

Group plans for checkups, cleanings, glasses and more, offered through work.

Few or no health questions

Our specialty: guaranteed-issue and simplified-issue plans for people who need coverage most.

We work with Aflac, MassMutual, Guardian, National Life Group and many other carriers. Plan names, benefits and availability vary by carrier and state.
