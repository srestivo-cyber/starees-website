Had a checkup or screening this year?

Many supplemental plans, including many cancer and critical illness plans, pay a benefit just for getting a covered health screening. Most people don't realize how much their plan actually pays.

  • Get a covered screening, such as a mammogram, colonoscopy or blood work. Your brochure lists which tests count.
  • Note the date, the name of the test, and the doctor or lab.
  • Call or email the office. We check your policies and file it for you.

Every covered person counts. Check whether your plan pays for your spouse and children too.

Good questions we hear a lot

Do I need to know which policy pays for what?

No. Tell us what happened and we'll check every benefit on every policy you have with us.

Can I just send pictures of my bills?

Yes. Clear phone photos are fine. Email them or fax them to the office.

I already filed a claim myself. Can you still help?

Yes. Tell us anyway. We'll look for anything the filing missed.

My employer offers these plans. Who do I call with questions?

Call us. We service the policies we enroll all year, not just at enrollment time.

I left my job. What happens to my policy?

You can keep it and pay for it directly, or let it go. Call us and we'll walk you through it.

Do you offer life insurance outside of work?

Yes. We shop many life insurance carriers to find the right fit. Call the office to start a quote.
