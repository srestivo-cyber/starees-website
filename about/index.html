Why we exist

"In my twenties I had cancer three times, every other year, and in the end I was told I was terminal with six months to live. One fateful day after treatment, I left the hospital on my way home and, while sitting at a light, was in a serious auto accident. For almost 15 months I was out of work, alternating every few months between cancer treatment and surgery from the accident.

I was fortunate to recover from both. Having managed law firms, I had good benefits, and I had also bought several voluntary benefit policies, which most people in their twenties don't do. With a legal background, I was constantly navigating how to get the service and receive the benefits I deserved, and overcoming the service challenges. By the third round I had become proficient at the pitfalls, but it was arduous nonetheless. I realized there had to be a better way.

I wrote to a Fortune 500 insurance carrier about my experience: grateful for the benefits I had, and with the changes I thought they should make. They immediately offered me a position. That led to several management positions, including traveling throughout the United States as a trainer. It soon became clear I should become independent and start my own brokerage.

For a long time, with my medical history, I was the dirty penny nobody wanted. At 32 I had stopped all my medical care and looked like the picture of health. To my doctors, I was a miracle. Still, I couldn't get insurance anywhere. Once I started my agency the offers came, but either the costs were catastrophic or the benefits were worthless. It left me feeling uninsurable.

So in my own search I naturally looked for coverage with no health questions, guaranteed issue and simplified issue, that actually paid benefits when you needed them. I found a lot of people wanted the same thing, even people who were perfectly healthy. They want the satisfaction of knowing they'll actually receive the benefits they pay into.

26 years later, we may not understand everything a client is going through, but we are certainly empathetic."
