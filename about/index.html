Why we exist

Sal Restivo started the office after battling cancer for the third time and then going through a serious auto accident. Living through those claims taught him there had to be a better way.

Before going independent he held management positions at Aflac. In 2026 the office marks its 25th anniversary, and many clients have been with us for fifteen to twenty-five years.
